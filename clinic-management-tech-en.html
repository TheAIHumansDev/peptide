Precision data

Results are easier to trust when they are numbers captured the same way every time. Every reading is stored with the conditions it was taken in, so progress is something you can show, not something you describe.

Camera-based vitals · rPPG

Pulse, breathing and blood pressure trend from a 30-second face scan.

Each heartbeat changes skin color by amounts too small to see. The camera reads that signal from the forehead and cheeks to estimate heart rate, heart-rate variability and breathing rate, plus a blood pressure trend over time.

FAQ

What clinics ask us.

Who is Clinic Management Tech for?

Aesthetic medicine and aesthetic dermatology clinics, medical spas, longevity and wellness practices, and any practice that treats the face or body and wants to measure and follow up on its patients.

How accurate are the measurements?

Accuracy comes from consistency. The app only saves a capture when light, distance and head pose are within tolerance, and it uses the same 468-point landmark model every time, so two visits are measured the same way. Each reading is stored with its capture conditions and a quality score, and weak captures are retaken.

Can it really measure blood pressure and breathing through the camera?

The camera reads the tiny color changes in the skin caused by each heartbeat (remote photoplethysmography, or rPPG). From that signal it estimates heart rate, heart-rate variability and breathing rate. Blood pressure is shown as an estimated trend over time. These are wellness estimates that help the professional; clinical blood pressure should be confirmed with a validated cuff.

Does the facial analysis replace a medical assessment?

No. The 468-point analysis is a support tool that helps the professional document and explain proportions, symmetry and skin. The assessment and the treatment decision always belong to the doctor.

Can we add lab results and longevity tests?

Yes. Blood panels, biological-age and epigenetic reports can be uploaded to the patient record, so they appear on the same timeline as the facial and vitals data.

Do my patients need to download anything?

Each patient gets access to their app on their phone, where they see their appointments, progress, routine and messages with the clinic.

What does Savia, the AI assistant, do?

Savia talks to your patients. She explains their analysis in plain language, sends aftercare and routine guidance, books their next check-up and flags anything urgent, like pain or swelling, to your team right away. She never gives a diagnosis, and your team can step into any conversation.

Can I use my own brand and colors?

Yes. We set up the platform with your clinic's identity so your patients recognize it as yours.
